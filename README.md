# Line Two

> Cut through the noise. Talk to the real person behind the avatar — eventually.

## The Problem

The digital ecosystem has become unusably noisy.

- **DMs are closed.** Creators, founders, and public figures lock their inboxes because spam has drowned out everything else.
- **Cold outreach is dead.** Cold emails rarely get opened, let alone answered.
- **Genuine people can't reach genuine people.** The signal — a real fan, a real opportunity, a real connection — is buried under an avalanche of noise.

The people worth reaching are unreachable, and the people trying to reach them have no honest channel.

## The Idea

**Line Two** lets anyone talk to a person's AI avatar — via chat or voice — as a trusted front door to the real human behind it.

The avatar isn't a replacement for the person. It's a filter, a concierge, and a listening layer. It gives fans and well-meaning strangers a genuine way in, while protecting the person's time and attention.

### How it works

**Side 1 — The People's Directory**

Invited people create their avatar through a deep questionnaire covering:

- Where they were born, their upbringing and education
- Their work, passions, and hobbies
- Their ideologies and worldview
- The kind of people they *want* to talk to

This shapes an avatar that genuinely reflects who they are.

**Side 2 — The Conversations**

Anyone on the platform can talk to any avatar via chat or voice. Every conversation is real signal — a person who cared enough to show up and talk.

**The Loop — Curated Summaries**

This is the interesting part. Instead of forwarding raw transcripts, Line Two sends avatar owners a **curated digest** of their conversations — daily, weekly, or on their preferred cadence. They can see:

- Who talked to their avatar
- What was interesting, thoughtful, or worth their time

If a conversation seems interesting, the owner can **connect with that person on the platform** — turning a chat with an AI into a real human relationship.

## Why This Works

Avatars flip the economics of attention.

- For **notable people**: every conversation is pre-filtered by an agent that knows them, so only the best rise to the surface. Their inbox stays empty; their signal stays strong.
- For **everyone else**: there's finally an honest way to be heard. You're not a cold email in a spam folder — you're a conversation an agent genuinely understood and championed.

## The Road Ahead

- **Spaces** — curated rooms where owners meet the people their avatars connected them with, taking online conversations into real community.
- Deeper agent capabilities — avatars that not only talk, but actively scout, introduce, and organize the right people around the right person.
